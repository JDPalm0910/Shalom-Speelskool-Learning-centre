Our Classes & Programmes

Learning experiences designed for every stage of early childhood.

Little Lambs

Gross motor development is important for preschoolers because it develops their large muscles, balance, coordination, and body control. It helps children run, jump, climb, and balance, builds their self-confidence, and lays an important foundation for learning and everyday activities.

Little Explorers

Art at preschool stimulates children's creativity, imagination, and self-expression. Through painting, drawing, cutting, pasting, and working with different materials, they develop fine motor skills, hand-eye coordination, concentration, and self-confidence. Art gives children the opportunity to explore their own ideas and proudly share their creations

Little Stars

Fine motor development is important for preschoolers because it strengthens the small muscles in their hands and fingers. It helps children hold pencils, draw, cut, fasten buttons, and eventually learn to write more easily. It also promotes hand-eye coordination, concentration, and independence.
